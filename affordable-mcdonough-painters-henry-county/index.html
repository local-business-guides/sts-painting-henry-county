Make the scope easier to understand

Before requesting an estimate, take a few minutes to organize the details. A short checklist can help you explain priorities and make sure the conversation covers the right surfaces:
- List the rooms and exterior areas you want painted or treated.
- Identify damage, peeling paint, or other conditions you’ve noticed.
- Separate must-do work from changes you may want to consider later.
- Ask what preparation, repairs, and related services are included.
- Confirm which areas are outside the proposed scope.

Try not to decide based on a vague description of the work. Two painting plans may sound similar while addressing different rooms or preparation needs. Ask for the scope to be explained plainly, and bring up questions before the project starts. That can help prevent confusion about what you expected the painter to do.
